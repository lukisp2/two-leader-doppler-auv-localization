IET RSN-2026-10-0375 revision experiment: difficult initial formation conditions
Protocol version 1.0, specified 2026-11-02 before any new simulation outcome.
This is a reviewer-requested supplementary stress evaluation, not a replacement
of the previously frozen 100-scenario campaigns or a formal qualification test.

Question
How do the unchanged proportional and delay-aware post-release controllers
perform in the complete acquisition-to-tracking loop from large initial
formation errors and large heading misalignment within the stated domain?

Selection (independent of all outcomes)
Use the original frozen nominal initialization and prior, including follower
distance 120-350 m from the initial leader centroid. Reconstruct or sample only
the initial state for seeds 52000-52999 in ascending order. Retain the first 50
seeds satisfying BOTH:
  norm(pF(0)-pF_des(0)) >= 350 m;
  abs(wrap180(yawF(0)-atan2(vC_y,vC_x))) >= 120 degrees.
The desired point is the original point 120 m behind the leader centroid.
Retain the full screened seed list and initial geometry. No simulation outcome
or estimated lock time participates in selection. If fewer than 50 qualify,
stop before campaign execution and revise the prospective design explicitly.
Check the available prior experiment records for selected-seed reuse; abort if
any selected seed is known to have been evaluated. The sealed 50000-50999 range
is prohibited. Smoke checks use only the previously opened seed 49566 and/or
49591 and are not included among the 50 stress scenarios.

Paired arms: 50 scenarios x 2 controllers = 100 complete episodes
  A: the unchanged baseline proportional tracker;
  B: the unchanged delay-aware tracker from the published controller follow-up.
Both use both Doppler links, the same information-guided acquisition, full-history
estimator and audited evidence gate, delayed first-order plant and no current.
The estimator prior, gains, thresholds, action set, timing, search budget and
sensor-noise settings are unchanged. The reference/treatment use identical
initial state and exogenous sensor-noise realization within a seed. Current is
not applied in either arm; the existing campaign remains the current comparison.
Do not use simulator truth in a controller, planner, estimator or gate.

Frozen settings and endpoints
Use the published 440 s horizon, 220 two-second actions, two 4096-point sweeps,
48 local starts, raw estimator, uniform-radius search, and default planner/gate.
Use the original terminal, Tail80 and Dwell15 joint-success definitions, 7 m
localization tolerance and 8 m formation tolerance. Keep every selected episode,
including no-TRACK episodes and finite-horizon failures. Do not extend the horizon
after observing a failure or tune a parameter on these data.
Report counts/rates and paired differences, terminal localization and formation
errors (median/p95/max and finite counts), first-TRACK time conditional on lock
with its denominator, TRACK saturation/curvature with availability, and truth-
invalid release/TRACK counts. These are descriptive stress results. No new
acceptance margin or hypothesis of guaranteed success is imposed retroactively.

Integrity and execution
Freeze source hashes, this protocol and the complete selected seed list before
the first full stress episode. Record exact runtime/interpreter and configurations.
Run seeds and arms sequentially. Save each arm's JSON and full trace immediately;
provide progress, completed/total and measured-runtime ETA in a readable log and
machine-readable progress.json. Resume must check frozen hashes/configuration.
Validate pairing and bitwise pre-first-TRACK trace identity excluding wall time;
report any mismatch as an integrity error, not a scientific outcome. Verify one
smoke reference reproduces the unmodified runner. Do not silently patch frozen
source files. New runner glue may explicitly admit the new seed list while
preserving the sealed-range guard.

Interpretation limits
This conditions on difficult starts within the original correct-prior domain.
It is not a test of out-of-prior initialization, greater acoustic range, random
nominal population success, unobserved current or calibrated vehicle dynamics.
Initial distance from the leader centroid is not initial formation error. Large
initial error can delay acquisition and leave less time for formation recovery;
separate localization and formation failures in the analysis.
